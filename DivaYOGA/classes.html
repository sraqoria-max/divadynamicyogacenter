Kids Yoga

Fun, interactive, and disciplined yoga sessions designed specifically for growing children. Enhances memory power, focus, spinal posture, body flexibility, and emotional calmness through mindful play and breathing.

Adult Yoga

🌅 Mon & Wed: 05:30 – 06:30 AM 🌙 Sat & Sun: 06:00 – 07:00 PM

Comprehensive yoga for adults combining core conditioning, mobility routines, back pain relief, and restorative sequences. Customised to individual body types and modern working lifestyles.
