Mudras & The Five Elements

A clear guide to key hand mudras and the five elemental principles used in yoga to improve focus, energy and emotional balance.

Introduction

What Are Mudras?

Mudras are yogic hand gestures used to influence the flow of energy in the body. Each finger represents one of the five elements — Space, Air, Fire, Water and Earth.

When fingers touch or press in specific ways, these elements come into balance, affecting focus, breath, mood and overall wellbeing. They are subtle yet profoundly effective tools used throughout our classes.

Finger to Element Mapping

👍 Thumb Fire
☝️ Index Air
🖕 Middle Space / Ether
💍 Ring Earth
🤙 Little Water
Practice Guide

Key Mudras We Use

Six essential mudras incorporated across our classes — each targeting specific energy flows.

💨
Gyan Mudra

Index finger touches the thumb.

🌀 Element: Air

✓ Improves focus, memory, calmness

📍 Used in: Meditation, Pranayama

💧
Prana Mudra

Thumb touches ring and little finger.

🌀 Element: Water + Earth

✓ Boosts vitality, reduces fatigue, increases immunity

📍 Used in: Energy-building practices

🌍
Apana Mudra

Thumb touches middle and ring finger.

🌀 Element: Earth + Water

✓ Improves digestion, detox, hormonal balance

📍 Used in: Therapy, digestive support practices

🌬️
Vayu Mudra

Index finger folded and pressed lightly with the thumb.

🌀 Element: Air

✓ Reduces anxiety, restlessness, joint discomfort

📍 Used in: Mobility, relaxation phases

✨
Shunya Mudra

Middle finger pressed gently by the thumb.

🌀 Element: Space (Ether)

✓ Helps with ear pressure, imbalance, mental lightness

📍 Used in: Quiet calming practices

🌌
Dhyana Mudra

Hands in lap, right over left, palms facing upward.

🌀 Element: Space

✓ Deepens meditation, enhances stillness

📍 Used in: Meditation, Savasana

The Five Elements

Yoga understands the human system through five natural elements. Our classes integrate breath, posture and mudras to harmonise all five.

🌌
Space (Ether)
Expansion, openness Imbalance: Overthinking, lack of clarity Balance: Meditation, Dhyana Mudra
💨
Air
Movement, breathing Imbalance: Anxiety, instability Balance: Mobility flows, Vayu Mudra
🔥
Fire
Heat, transformation Imbalance: Anger, stress, acidity Balance: Core strengthening, breath control
💧
Water
Fluidity, emotions Imbalance: Emotional heaviness, stiffness Balance: Flowing sequences, Prana Mudra
🌍
Earth
Stability, grounding Imbalance: Weakness, insecurity Balance: Standing postures, Apana Mudra
