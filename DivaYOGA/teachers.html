Teaching Philosophy

Guided by These Principles

🛡️

Safety Always First

Every modification, every adjustment and every cue is filtered through the lens of student safety and gradual, sustainable progression.

📐

Precision Over Speed

Correct alignment and mindful movement always take priority over the number of repetitions or the pace of the class.

📈

Measurable Progress

Clear milestones and weekly check-ins ensure students always know they are moving forward — building confidence along with strength.
